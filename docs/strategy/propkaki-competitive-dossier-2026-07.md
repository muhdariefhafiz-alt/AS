# PropKaki Competitive Dossier

Date: 2026-07-27 · Basis: 30 logged-in product surfaces (10 extracted 27 Jul morning, 20 this afternoon), cross-checked against our production database. Owner goals: threat level, learnings to steal, features to steal, data sources; extended with monetization pressure points, execution quality, legal exposure, AI-citation battlefield, coexistence.

## Headline verdict

**Threat: 6.5/10 today, direction rising.** (Adversarial steelman rated 7/10; discount for their focus risk across 20+ surfaces and monetization immaturity.)

**The single most important finding, and a correction to our earlier read:** PropKaki's CEA Agent Directory has REPLICATED our raw data layer. Not aggregate, not quarterly-lagged: 37,876 agents tracked, 1.28M per-agent-attributed deals (vs our 1.34M), side-of-deal splits (seller/buyer/landlord/tenant), segment splits, 27-town leaderboards, time windows down to last month, and a deals-distribution histogram. The "our data is unique" story is dead. What they have NOT replicated: any trust layer (no score, no recency weighting, no verification, no claims, no reviews, no honesty flags), any per-agent profile depth on-page, and any seller-matching or agent-office mechanics. Their headline ranking is raw lifetime volume with rentals summed into sales, the exact skew we corrected in AgentScore.

**Consequence:** our moat was never the CSV; it is the interpretation + honesty + matching + agent-office layer, and at 3 claimed agents that layer is mostly unbuilt. The competition is now a race: their distribution (content velocity + WhatsApp assistant + free-tool funnel) versus our trust depth (AgentScore, verified claims, seller shortlists, dashboard). The steelman's decisive move for them is attaching claiming to utility (Operate tools) and pointing their consumer funnel at agent selection; our claim flow + Google sign-in shipping this week is exactly the right race to be running.

## What PropKaki is (their goal)

A property AI assistant company: (1) free commodity-data tools + thousands of programmatic SEO pages capture consumer and AI-citation traffic at near-zero marginal cost; (2) every page funnels into an owned conversational channel (app chat + WhatsApp assistant); (3) monetization is aimed at agents via the Operate SaaS suite (Paperwork, Prospecting, Listings, Slides, Videos). Notably: across all 20 captures not a single price was visible. Their monetization is immature; that is our window.

## Execution quality (their weakness pattern)

Fast and sloppy. Automation without review, on their flagship surfaces: valuation demo with a stretch offer below the opening offer and a self-contradicting verdict; 27 vs 16,095 unsold units on one page; unit-mix table summing 3,336 against a stated 2,203 total; Q1-2017 town table beside a Q4-2025 hero; "Queestown" typo town; Propnex agent count differing on the same page; sentiment tag calling an AGO lapses report Positive; 1-deal "top agent" monthly leaderboard winners. They ship pages per hour and errors per page. Our honesty rails are a real differentiator against this, and factual comparative content writes itself.

## Legal exposure map (asymmetric leverage)

- Scraped portal listings (PropertyGuru, 99.co, EdgeProp) load-bear FIVE tools incl. valuation comps and the 60k active-listings claim: the exact walled-garden ToS exposure we ruled DOA. One enforcement letter collapses the pillar.
- Listing Video Assistant re-renders portal photos: direct photo-copyright exposure (our Etalage fotorechten lesson).
- Floor plans digitised from developer brochures: copyrighted works, no licensing statement.
- We depend on none of these. Their strongest surfaces stand on fragile ground; ours stand on government data and first-party demand.

## Coexistence scenarios

Low. They court the same agent wallet (Operate suite) and the same seller decision moments. No channel play available; treat as primary strategic competitor in SG alongside portals.

## Reading the matrix

- Threat distribution: 16 tools at 2 or 2.5, one at 3, two at 4. The only 4s are the two CEA-records plays (Agent Directory, Agency Finder), i.e. the only surfaces that touch FC's per-agent attribution wedge. Everything else is property-centric.
- The consumer analytics stack (rows 1-13, 20) is uniformly free, uniformly funnels to the same app/WhatsApp assistant, and sits almost entirely on commodity government data. It builds mindshare upstream of agent selection but attacks nothing FC monetizes directly.
- The agent OPERATE suite (rows 15-19) is the strategic tell: PropKaki is competing for the agent's daily workflow, the same surface as FC's agent dashboard, before it has an agent-comparison product. Slides Assistant (3/5) is the one where FC's transaction moat makes a strictly better version possible.
- Depth is bimodal: genuinely craftsmanlike where government data allows (Tower View, Landed Directory, HDB Pricing Model) and thin everywhere the value depends on unproven AI output (Videos, Slides, chat entry) or manual editorial upkeep (Timeline, HDB Directory staleness).

# PropKaki Monetization Map

## Free (confirmed, no gate observed on any of the 20 captures)

Every captured surface is free at point of use, most explicitly branded "Free Tools" or "Free, from URA/CEA/HDB data":

- All consumer calculators and analytics: Valuation Assistant, Timeline Planner, Financial Planner, HDB Pricing Model, Profitability Model, Comparison Assistant, Tower View, Transaction Finder
- All programmatic-SEO directories: Landed, HDB, Condo, New-launches hub, CEA Agent Directory, Agency Finder
- Agent-side surfaces as captured: Agent Market Overview, Listing Planner (free-tool feel), and the marketing pages for Paperwork, Video, and Slides Assistants

No prices, tiers, or paywalls were visible anywhere in the 20 captures. Not one.

## App-gated (the universal funnel)

The real gate is not money, it is channel migration. Every single page, all 20, runs the same three CTAs: "Ask PropKaki" (in-app AI chat), "Try on WhatsApp" (WhatsApp assistant), "Keep going in the PropKaki app". Depth is deliberately deferred behind this gate:

- Agent Directory defers all per-agent profile depth to the AI assistant
- Directories embed a pre-answered chat teaser ("around $598k, high floors nearer $660k") as the bridge
- Tool completions are upsold at the moment of success ("Got a valuation, now pressure-test it"; "Made the video, now arm the conversation")
- Accounts exist (logged-in captures with avatar), so per-user gating infrastructure is in place even though no gate was hit

## Premium / paid (inferred, never shown)

- A "Pricing" page exists in the footer of every capture; its contents were never rendered in any of the 20 reports, so actual price points are unknown
- A "For Agents" page plus the OPERATE suite (Paperwork, Prospecting, Listings, Slides, Videos) with recurring "For agents, in the PropKaki app" banners is the obvious paid tier
- Two tools tease with collapsed FAQs ("Is PropKaki Paperwork Assistant free to use?", "Is the Slides Assistant free to use?"), the classic freemium wording pattern
- No consumer premium tier, no ads, no lead-selling, and no per-listing fees were observed anywhere

## Apparent revenue model

A three-stage funnel: (1) free commodity-data tools and programmatic SEO capture consumer and AI-citation traffic at near-zero marginal cost; (2) every visit is converted into an owned conversational channel (app account or WhatsApp thread), building daily habit; (3) monetization lands on the agent side via an Operate SaaS workspace, where agents pay for workflow utility (decks, videos, listing packs, forms), not for leads or prominence.

Notably absent: any lead-gen marketplace, any pay-for-placement, any commission participation. The consumer side appears to be pure acquisition and mindshare.

## Implication for FC

PropKaki's paying customer is the same agent FC's dashboard wants to retain, and their retention hooks are high-frequency chores (forms, slides, listing copy) that FC's dashboard currently lacks, while FC's paying surface (S$99/299 subscriptions tied to AgentScore, claims, and seller shortlists) is untouched by anything they have shipped. The exposed flank runs the other way too: FC could bolt PropKaki-style chore utilities onto the dashboard faster than PropKaki can build a trust layer (verified claims, attributed track records, recency-weighted scores) under its directories. The monetization race is for agent workflow habit, not for consumer data, and neither side has closed it yet.

# PropKaki 12-Month Attack Plan Against FairComparisons

## Situation read

FairComparisons' entire defensible position is one thing: per-agent CEA transaction attribution (1.34M records) wrapped in a trust layer (AgentScore with honesty flags, verified claims, seller shortlists) plus an agent dashboard (inbox, planner, deal radar). Everything else about FC is weak right now: roughly 3 claimed agents, no consumer AI assistant, a content cadence measured in pages per week against PropKaki's pages per hour, and a seller funnel with no distribution engine feeding it.

PropKaki already holds four assets FC cannot match in 12 months:

1. **The CEA Agent Directory** (30k+ agents, 1.28M attributed deals, side-of-deal splits, town leaderboards, data fresh to last month). This already neutralizes FC's raw-data uniqueness. The data wedge is gone; only FC's interpretation layer remains.
2. **The Agency Directory** (all 788 CEA agencies) as the B2B door-opener.
3. **The consumer AI assistant** (Ask PropKaki web chat + WhatsApp), an owned messaging channel FC has no equivalent of, fed by every free tool and directory page.
4. **Content velocity + the Operate suite** (36 editorial reviews in 48 hours, thousands of programmatic pages across HDB/condo/landed/new-launch, plus Paperwork, Prospecting, Listings, Slides, Videos for agents).

The plan is to make agent selection a feature of PropKaki's existing funnel rather than a destination, and to make the Operate suite the agent's daily habit before FC's dashboard reaches double-digit claimed agents.

## Phase 1 (Months 1-2): Fix the directory so it survives scrutiny

FC's only credible counterattack is "PropKaki's rankings are naive volume counts." Close that door first.

- Replace lifetime-cumulative rankings with a recency-weighted score: 12-month deals weighted over lifetime, sales separated from rentals in the headline metric, minimum-n suppression on short-window leaderboards (kill the "town winner with 3 deals" embarrassment).
- Ship 30k programmatic per-agent profile pages (name + CEA number + agency + segment splits + town activity + deals histogram position), each with question-styled FAQ blocks tuned for AI citation ("How many HDB flats has [agent] sold in Tampines?"). Every agent-name Google query should land on PropKaki, not FC.
- Fix the visible logic bugs in the valuation and pricing tools (stretch-offer inversion, upper=typical PSF, Q1 2017 town table). These are the ammunition FC's honesty positioning would use in comparative content. Remove it before escalating.

Cost: mostly data-engineering on data PropKaki already ingests. No new sources needed.

## Phase 2 (Months 2-4): Point the consumer funnel at agent selection

This is the decisive move. PropKaki owns the moments upstream of agent choice: valuation, timeline planning, affordability, profitability, listing research. FC has to acquire sellers cold; PropKaki already has them mid-decision.

- Add an agent-shortlist exit to every consumer tool: valuation result ends with "3 agents who closed the most sales in this block/district in the last 12 months, seller-side"; timeline planner's Sell track inserts "week 1: pick your agent, here is your data-backed shortlist"; financial planner's Selling persona does the same.
- Make "Which agent should I use to sell in [town]?" a first-class WhatsApp assistant intent, answered from the directory with named agents, deal counts, and side-of-deal evidence. The agency-finder mock chat already prototypes exactly this; ship it for real.
- Launch ~30 programmatic "Best agents to sell your HDB in [town]" and district equivalents, recency-ranked, refreshed monthly, FAQ-loaded for AI citation. This directly contests the query space FC's seller funnel depends on.

Result by month 4: a seller can go from "what is my flat worth" to a named, evidence-backed shortlist without ever hearing of FC. FC's seller funnel is starved at the source.

## Phase 3 (Months 3-6): Win the claimed-agent race with the Operate suite

FC has 3 claimed agents. That is not a moat, it is a head start PropKaki can erase in one quarter.

- Attach claiming to utility, not ego: to put your face, WhatsApp number, and reviews on your (already live) PropKaki profile, claim it. To unlock unlimited Paperwork forms, Slides decks, and Listing Videos, claim it. Every Operate tool becomes a claim funnel; agents claim to get work done, not to be compared.
- Run the leaderboards as ego-bait outbound: monthly "You ranked #4 for seller-side HDB deals in Punggol" messages to top agents per town (public CEA data, their own numbers). Claim to display the badge.
- Add the one Operate feature FC could beat PropKaki with, before FC ships it: an auto-generated "My track record" slide/page from the directory data, insertable into every Slides pitch deck. That is the agent's why-me artifact, powered by the same attribution data, and it makes PropKaki the place where an agent's record lives.
- Target: 500-1,500 claimed agents by month 6. At that point FC's "verified claims" story reads as the smaller, later copy of PropKaki's.

## Phase 4 (Months 6-9): Monetize and lock in

- Pricing: free profile + capped Operate usage; paid tier (positioned against SG agents' high commissions, they can afford premium) for unlimited Paperwork/Slides/Videos, lead routing from the consumer shortlists, and profile enhancements that never alter ranking (copy FC's presence-not-prominence stance explicitly; it removes FC's ethics differentiation).
- Agency-level deals via the Agency Directory: sell Operate seats and recruitment-facing agency pages to the big 5 (Propnex, ERA, Huttons, OrangeTee, SRI). One agency deal onboards more agents than FC's total claimed base by orders of magnitude.
- Route seller shortlist contacts through WhatsApp with response-time tracking. Now PropKaki has behavioral data (responsiveness, conversion) that public CEA records cannot provide and FC cannot replicate without volume.

## Phase 5 (Months 9-12): Kill sequence

1. **Query blanket**: 30k agent pages + town best-agent pages + existing directory hubs outrank and out-cite FC on effectively every agent-selection query in both Google and AI answers. FC's thin cadence cannot contest this surface.
2. **Funnel interception**: every seller researching price on PropKaki gets a shortlist before FC can reach them. FC's funnel volume flatlines.
3. **Agent poach**: any agent FC does claim gets targeted Operate outreach ("your record, plus the tools you use daily, free"). FC's dashboard has inbox/planner/deal-radar but no daily chore-killer like Paperwork; habit beats intent.
4. **Narrative flip**: publish a methodology page contrasting recency-weighted, side-attributed rankings with "static score sites," pre-empting FC's honesty attack by out-transparencing it.

By month 12 FC is left defending an interpretation layer (AgentScore nuance, honesty flags) that consumers cannot evaluate, on traffic it no longer receives, with a claimed base that lost the land grab.

## What FC could do that would hurt this plan

Ship a consumer AI shortlist assistant and a track-record deck generator inside its dashboard in the next 90 days, and land one mid-size agency deal before PropKaki's claim flow launches. The window is roughly one quarter.

## Threat rating: 7/10

The data moat is already neutralized (the directory exists, is fresh, and is attributed per agent, including side-of-deal). Distribution, AI channel, and agent tooling all favor PropKaki. What keeps this below 8-9: PropKaki is an everything-platform with visible quality debt (contradictory demos, stale tables, thin-n leaderboards), no evidence yet of a claim/verification layer or lead routing, and agent matching is currently a footer link, not a strategy. The plan above requires focus PropKaki has not yet demonstrated.

## Rating-driving assumptions

- PropKaki chooses to prioritize agent matching: the rating assumes they point their consumer funnel and WhatsApp assistant at 'which agent should I use' within roughly 6 months; today it is aggregate stats plus a footer directory, and an everything-platform with 20+ surfaces may never focus here, which would drop the threat to 3-4.
- CEA public records confer no exclusivity: FC's 1.34M attributed records are replicable from the same free government feed (PropKaki's 1.28M directory proves it), so FC's only durable moat is the trust/claims/matching layer, which at ~3 claimed agents is effectively unbuilt; if FC secures a hard-to-copy trust asset (verified reviews at scale, agency partnerships) the calculus changes.
- Distribution beats data quality at the decision moment: sellers accept the first credible evidence-backed shortlist they encounter, so PropKaki's SEO/AI-citation velocity and owned WhatsApp channel outweigh its visible ranking naivete and demo bugs; if consumers or AI answer engines actually punish volume-count rankings and stale tables, FC's honesty layer becomes a real defense and the threat falls to 5.

## PropKaki Property Timeline Planner (propkaki.com/property-timeline-planner)
